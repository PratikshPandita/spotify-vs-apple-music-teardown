# Feature Analysis: Spotify vs. Apple Music

*Last checked: 9 October 2026.*

## Key differentiators

### 1. Podcasts and audio content
- **Spotify:** Treats podcasts, including video podcasts, as a core part of the main app.
- **Apple Music:** Focuses on music; podcasts live in a separate app, Apple Podcasts.

### 2. Audio quality
- **Spotify:** Offers lossless audio (up to 24-bit/44.1 kHz), but in India only on Premium Platinum (₹299). Standard and Student stream at up to about 320 kbps.
- **Apple Music:** Includes Lossless and Spatial Audio with Dolby Atmos in every plan at no extra cost. Hi-Res Lossless needs external equipment such as a USB DAC.

### 3. Discovery and curation
- **Spotify:** Leads on personalization, with features like Wrapped, Blend playlists and AI DJ, built on a heavy data infrastructure.
- **Apple Music:** Combines algorithmic and editorial playlists, with more emphasis on human curation. Recommendations surprise less often but stay consistently relevant.

### 4. Integration and ecosystem
- **Apple Music:** Works best with iPhone, Apple Watch, Siri and CarPlay.
- **Spotify:** Runs across Android, Windows, smart speakers and many other devices, without depending on one ecosystem.

### 5. Pace of new features
- **Spotify:** Launches new social, personalization and AI features often.
- **Apple Music:** Ships fewer but more polished updates, with a focus on audio quality.

---

## Summary
Spotify wins on breadth: personalization, social features and podcasts. Apple Music wins on audio quality for the price and on ecosystem integration. In India, lossless audio costs ₹139 a month on Apple Music and ₹299 on Spotify.
